# Oga at the Top — Complete Implementation Action Plan

Date: 5 October 2026

## Scope and execution agreement

This plan consolidates the entire review conversation into one immediate implementation scope. Every workstream below belongs to the current redesign. There are no deferred phases, future-feature buckets or pilot-only substitutes for the agreed scope. Dependencies establish execution order; they do not postpone features to another release. Work can proceed concurrently where dependencies permit.

The requested deliverable in this turn is the action plan. This document does not claim that the game changes have already been implemented. Publishing, release access and deployment must follow the applicable project instructions and authorization at execution time.

The design objective is a continuing country whose governments change its capabilities, distribution of power, expectations and future decisions. The player authors a political history and then governs inside its consequences.

## Completion rules

- A consequential decision must create a durable change, commitment or episode, with a visible route to its later outcome.
- Distinguish verified events, allegations, official claims, forecasts and observed outcomes.
- Effects remain inspectable. Uncertainty concerns evidence, ranges and future outcomes; it must not excuse misleading descriptions.
- A recurrence must contain a material development. Unchanged conditions belong in status views, not repeated interruptions.
- Success must deliver relief, new capability or a changed country. It must not merely generate another equivalent demand.
- Support several defensible governing programmes and credible self-interested strategies. Do not force identical win rates or one universally optimal route.
- Preserve authored satire, deterministic simulation, reproducible seeds and meaningful player judgement.
- Every workstream includes engine behaviour, authored content, interface, persistence and verification where applicable. A menu or headline alone does not count as implementation.

## 01 — Reliable history, succession and economic foundations

Actions:

- Establish durable identities for people, institutions, assets, agreements and episodes. Office and individual identity must be separate.
- Introduce a world calendar that continues across presidencies, with presidency-relative dates for term mechanics. Migrate existing founding dates, deadlines and durations correctly.
- Fix inherited institutions producing negative performance after turn resets.
- Preserve repeal state, selected policy approaches, active cases, deferred effects, unfinished projects, contracts and financial obligations through succession.
- Attribute adviser records to actual individuals, not the office they occupy. A replacement starts their own forecasting record.
- Evaluate forecasts when their stated observation horizon arrives, against realised outcomes. Never score future scheduled effects as already achieved.
- Replace narrative assertions derived from unrelated gauges with specific evidence or appropriately attributed allegations. The identity-data repair must not invent a sale of data from generic scandal heat.
- Preserve nonfinancial misconduct in investigations, archives and endings. A protected or celebrated retirement may coexist with a documented record of abuse.
- Reconcile inconsistent monetary units, double counting, missing liabilities and derived gauges with their underlying accounts.

Acceptance:

- A three-government history preserves functioning institutions, repealed policies, live cases, commitments and currency exposures without calendar corruption.
- Replacing an adviser does not transfer their accuracy record. Delayed predictions remain pending until observable.
- Endings distinguish reputation, legal exposure, personal wealth and documented misconduct.

## 02 — A new transition into office and ceremonial certificate

Actions:

- Replace the overloaded certificate form with a transition experience: country dossier, identity/background, route to power, mandate, completed certificate, oath, private briefing and first exercise of power.
- Keep all existing inheritances, with the outgoing government's claims, verified conditions, unresolved obligations, a worthwhile functioning asset and named holders of leverage.
- Separate professional background from electoral coalition and financing. Include establishment, broad coalition, mobilisation and continuity routes, with differentiated commitments and support.
- Make the financier a deliberate choice or a clearly explained consequence. Include small-contributor support with its own strengths and costs.
- Give party identity political substance through programme, constituencies and coalition agreements rather than editable names alone.
- Retain the four-point mandate, expressed as understandable public outcomes. Show relevant reform approaches and optional detail without requiring the player to understand seventeen tracks first.
- Allow an optional explicit governing constraint, such as protecting investigations or ruling out a particular financing approach. Store it as a commitment.
- Present the certificate as a completed ceremonial instrument with validated identity, home state, party, world year and unique historical reference. Resolve inconsistent oath/certificate ordering and silent identity defaults.
- Improve typography, spacing, fictional seal and document composition. Move policy configuration and biographies into appropriate documents.
- Show starting effects accurately, including which choices survive scenario and inheritance application.
- Provide a compact route for experienced players through the same meaningful choices.

Acceptance:

- Every inheritance has a complete opening. The Reformer’s Handover is a required detailed acceptance scenario, not a limit on implementation.
- A new player makes informed commitments and sees their first consequences without studying the complete reform catalogue.
- No consequential choice is silently selected or overwritten without explanation.

## 03 — Player-selected government and persistent talent

Actions:

- Let the President appoint the initial government from a proposed slate that can be accepted, altered or left partly vacant.
- Choose the running mate at the appropriate electoral point; select Chief of Staff, Finance, major ministers and available institutional appointments directly.
- Contextualise the first Finance appointment through a real payment, conflicting information and political expectations.
- Let coalition agreements constrain appointments through visible negotiations, not invisible forced choices.
- Build a shared pool of named candidates with careers, expertise, relationships, previous decisions and role-specific suitability.
- Make candidates recruitable, unavailable, retained, dismissed, promoted or recruited by successors and opponents. Develop deputies into credible future candidates.
- Implement exceptional candidates with distinctive capabilities and explicit acceptance conditions: authority, independence, protected funding, specialist teams, recruitment effort or political concessions.
- Include excellent candidates without compulsory hidden disasters. Brilliance still depends on resources and organisational support.
- Remove automatic reliance on generic loyalist/technocrat/party replacement categories as the full depth of recruitment.

Acceptance:

- Two governments appointing different teams encounter different delivery, advice and political consequences.
- Exceptional recruits change available capabilities, not just ratings. Their conditions persist and breaches have concrete reactions.
- Records and favours follow the relevant person rather than silently attaching to a replacement officeholder.

## 04 — Institutional agency, constitutional authority and courts

Actions:

- Give institutions mandates, legal powers, staff, funding, organisational culture, independence and oversight arrangements.
- Model independent action: investigations of allies, publication of unwelcome statistics, refusal to certify unfinished work or regulatory rejection of unsuitable projects.
- Support competent, timid, doctrinaire, industry-captured and procedurally slow institutions. Independence is not an automatic virtue score.
- Let strong leaders develop routines and successors that survive their departure.
- Define which presidential actions are executive orders, legislative matters, federal bargains or decisions of independent bodies.
- Implement entrenchment and constitutional provisions as persistent rules governing authority and successor constraints.
- Give judges legal philosophy, procedural standards, administrative ability, integrity and pressure responses. Cases consider authority, evidence, precedent and procedure rather than treating independence as opposition.
- Respect vacancies and the game's constitutional appointment processes; avoid wholesale presidential replacement of every court.

Acceptance:

- A capable independent institution can help the country and constrain its founder in the same campaign.
- A constitutional change alters future legal or governing behaviour. A court outcome explains its reasoning and relevant evidence.

## 05 — Requests, refusal, precise promises and favours

Actions:

- Store persistent requests with a specific object, requester, status, terms and history. Separate underlying ambitions from individual requests.
- Refusal closes the request. Renewed pressure requires a changed offer, appeal, threat, coalition move or other material development.
- Differentiate responses by motive, personality, leverage, legitimacy and explanation. Do not create a universal grudge from two unrelated refusals.
- Replace time-window rotation as the principal model of unresolved requests.
- Preserve promise objects. An unrelated concession must not fulfil a promise; substitutions require recipient agreement.
- Reconcile bilateral favours through explicit offset, partial settlement, forgiveness or negotiated replacement. Track remaining size and terms consistently for politicians and tycoons.
- Add contextual uses: withdraw a demand, broker contact, amend a provision, deliver an agreement, supply actual evidence, release a partner from a term, secure project support and forgive an obligation.
- Retain existing mobilisation, whipping, acceleration, money, investment, coverage, endorsement and witness-related uses where appropriate.
- Match assistance to actual influence and capability. Reject or explain uses that cannot produce an effect, such as accelerating an empty ministry brief.
- Represent bond purchases as financing with liabilities, distinct from donations and private investment.
- Make reconciliation address the dispute rather than automatically erasing all grievances after any grant.

Acceptance:

- A refused request cannot return unchanged as an unresolved ask.
- Mutual debts can settle partly or fully, with a traceable residual. Favour support has an identifiable target and effect.
- Precise commitments remain precise through grants, replacements, succession and negotiations.

## 06 — Programme coalitions, negotiated settlements and delegation

Actions:

- Give political actors positions on particular issues, with projects they pursue. General relationship warmth does not determine every position.
- Turn pacts into coordinated demands and delivery commitments with a shared objective.
- Negotiate legislation through provisions, implementation dates, geography, revenue distribution, oversight and appointments.
- Preserve concessions in the resulting agreement and implementation; passing a law begins delivery of its settlement.
- Delegate objectives, budget, authority, limits and reporting standards to ministers. Routine work proceeds without repeated presidential clicks.
- Escalate exceptions, missed targets, funding disputes and breaches. Evaluate written ministerial targets on their actual deadline, including the government’s contribution to failure.
- Build a unified commitments register for public promises, political settlements, targets, contracts, deadlines and financial obligations.

Acceptance:

- A major bill passes through negotiation and produces enforceable obligations plus differentiated later reactions.
- A six-month target creates an actual review with outcomes for delivery, failure, withheld funding or disputed evidence.

## 07 — Full content quality and consequence refresh

Actions:

- Audit every event, choice, outcome, recurrence and headline on the authoritative build. Historical inventory counts are not a cap or current truth.
- Organise electricity, fuel, universities, wages, procurement, industry, cabinet performance, security, coalition politics and succession into developing story families.
- Specify condition, episode, intervention, response, next development and exit for each family.
- Make repetition controls aware of interaction type, person, request, episode and recent experience, not just file ID.
- Review queued-event cooldown exceptions. Allow intentional scheduled beats while preventing accidental spam and duplicate unresolved episodes.
- Separate new decisions, progress updates, persistent conditions and closing reports in presentation.
- Audit verbs including investigate, prosecute, appoint, negotiate, implement and monitor. Deliver the promised mechanics or describe the action accurately as limited.
- Fix resource and history contradictions: depleted fuel reserves, dismissed people, completed reforms, settled disputes, committed funds and ongoing cases.
- Make debt crises, procurement disputes and project warnings evolve through changed participants, credibility, bargaining positions and options.
- Preserve stronger refinery and university chains, checking their assertions against actual state. Refresh repeated committee, denial, completion-percentage and ribbon-cutting jokes through specific mechanisms.
- Ensure competent advice does not automatically reveal the only sensible choice. Committees, talks and delay can have genuine uses with specific costs.

Acceptance:

- Long campaign traces include closure and quiet progress, not constant equivalent interruptions.
- Every recurring family has meaningful development and a resolution or transformation condition.
- Solved problems stop demanding the same decision; their descendants acknowledge the achievement.

## 08 — Information, evidence and political investigation

Actions:

- Give reports source, date, confidence, incentives and scope. Spending records, delivery claims and field findings answer different questions.
- Let investigations consume time and access, with opportunities to delay action, expose allies or contradict public announcements.
- Build authored mysteries around shortages, obstruction, delivery claims and patronage; retain deterministic resolution.
- Record what was known when deciding, what was forecast and what later occurred.
- Keep numeric effects inspectable, with uncertainty clearly attributed. Never replace explanation with hidden arbitrary punishment.

Acceptance:

- A player can reach different defensible conclusions from incomplete evidence and later trace why an assessment proved right or wrong.

## 09 — A coherent economy, currency and reserve model

Actions:

- Separate treasury cash, fiscal revenue/expenditure, private economic activity, external dollar flows and central-bank reserves.
- Label gross flows and baseline deviations correctly. Oil exports cannot become negative merely because earnings fall below a reference level.
- Separate domestic fiscal diversification from foreign-exchange earnings. Tax collection does not itself produce exports.
- Track reserves through explicit purchases, sales, official receipts, payments and valuation effects; avoid unexplained automatic allocation of private inflows.
- Show usable reserves, external financing pressure and intervention. Model reserve constraints under managed rates as well as pegs.
- Allow floating pressure to work through prices without mandatory reserve depletion, while explicitly accounting for any intervention or accumulation.
- Connect reserve adequacy to external obligations and confidence through clear mechanisms, not a universal high-reserves-equals-strong-currency bonus.
- Store foreign borrowing and principal exposure in their currency. Convert stock valuations and payments consistently; prevent derived debt recalculation erasing depreciation effects.
- Distinguish contractual rates, refinancing costs, inflation and exchange-rate valuation. Calculate service burden against actual revenue.
- Record diaspora bonds and other financing as liabilities with currency, terms and payment schedules. Verify announced proceeds against conversions.
- Give the foreign investment fund actual currency exposure, investment returns and valuation changes. Distinguish sovereign investments from readily usable reserves.
- Audit central-bank lending, securitisation and inflation effects. Do not imply that changing a debt label alone extinguishes created liquidity without a mechanism.
- Audit all deficits, arrears, savings, repayments and funds for conservation and double counting. Explain debt-service costs versus principal repayments.
- Audit fiscal federal transfers, oil benchmarks, subsidy pricing, import costs, debt thresholds and spending multipliers for economic meaning and gameplay.

Acceptance:

- Every cash receipt has a source; financing creates its corresponding obligation; disposal removes its corresponding ownership.
- Depreciation effects persist in foreign obligations. Changes in actual revenue affect service ratios.
- Tax reform, export growth and reserve accumulation can occur separately and connect only through explicit mechanisms.

## 10 — Separate tax policy from treasury management

Actions:

- Split the combined track into a taxation track covering who pays and how collection works, and a treasury track covering budgeting, spending, borrowing, saving and accountability.
- Reassign existing reforms deliberately: exemptions and collection under tax; remittance, public accounts, debt and expenditure controls under treasury.
- Add meaningful alternatives on consumption, income/property, incentives, informal-business regimes, wealthy noncompliance, rates versus collection and central versus coordinated administration.
- Make tax incidence, compliance, administrative cost and affected constituencies matter alongside revenue.
- Treasury success delivers predictable releases, credible budgets, fewer arrears, transparent procurement and manageable financing.
- Update mandates, prerequisites, ministerial responsibility, descriptions, balance scripts and migration for the split.

Acceptance:

- Strong collection with weak spending, and disciplined spending with a narrow tax base, are distinct playable conditions.

## 11 — Emergency liquidity and genuine asset transactions

Actions:

- Build an asset and ownership register covering disposable property, equipment, enterprise stakes and revenue rights, with valuation, use, encumbrances and income.
- Add a treasury emergency-funds view showing net proceeds, timing, conditions, costs and future obligations.
- Support surplus auctions, minority-stake sales, privatisation, leases/concessions, established tax-debt collection, stolen-fund recovery, savings withdrawals, emergency borrowing and expenditure reductions/deferrals.
- Distinguish orderly auctions, expedited competitive disposal and negotiated sales. Use bidders, reserve prices, diligence and payment completion; clean fair-price transactions must be possible.
- Remove sold ownership and future income. Prevent duplicate sales, disposal of already pledged interests and invented free assets.
- Treat essential services, occupied property and strategic assets differently from genuinely surplus holdings.
- Let proceeds arrive on actual schedules. A valuation or announcement is not immediately spendable cash.
- Track deferred contractual payments as obligations where appropriate. Repeated disposals leave a poorer balance sheet for successors.

Acceptance:

- A salary crisis can be resolved through a combination of timed measures with lasting, visible trade-offs.

## 12 — Big bets with distinctive delivery and operating consequences

Actions:

- Audit every big bet's promised output, costs, financing, prerequisites, failure causes, success effects and operating asset.
- Replace generic full-success/full-failure resolution where inappropriate with commissioning, partial operation, delays, scale changes and genuine failure.
- Separate essential operating conditions, cost/speed/scale factors, political acceptance and external uncertainty.
- Preserve warnings, partners, delay, rescue, revival and expansion, but connect interventions to the actual constraint they address.
- Connect refinery throughput to petrol imports and feedstock; steel/lithium to downstream capability; electricity exports to dollar contracts and domestic supply obligations; corridors to market access; gold purchases to valued reserve assets; healthcare to treatment access and avoided overseas spending.
- Give the constitution actual negotiated rules, diaspora finance actual debt and voting changes, census actual allocation consequences, and other victories their distinctive mechanism.
- Correct rail financing and all other narrative/unit mismatches.
- Replace decorative production records with observed quantities driving relevant outcomes. Management, maintenance, security and demand affect continuing performance.
- Reward achievement through capabilities, resolved constraints, beneficiaries, new opportunities and enduring recognition, alongside balanced numerical effects.

Acceptance:

- Each bet demonstrably changes a specific system beyond generic bonuses.
- Successful assets can operate, degrade, recover and be inherited. Partial delivery remains useful and historically distinguishable.

## 13 — Military institution and civilian control

Actions:

- Separate military capability from national security outcomes. Track readiness, personnel, command/intelligence and conduct through readable diagnostics.
- Add named defence leadership, operational commanders and logistics expertise with differing professional positions and political ties.
- Choose mission objectives, priority theatres, resources, conduct limits and success evidence; delegate operational execution.
- Connect pay, supplies, procurement, maintenance and dollar-dependent spare parts to actual accounts and readiness.
- Model local cooperation, civilian harm, intelligence and displacement. Tactical gains need not produce lasting security.
- Add procurement investigations, command disputes, appointments, leaks, ambition and resistance to political misuse. Coup risk is exceptional and causally grounded, not the institution's sole purpose.
- Add distinct military/logistics/corridor/repair/intelligence/demobilisation bets with partial outcomes and continuing support requirements.
- Preserve command transitions, contracts, deployments, professional improvements and unresolved abuses across governments.

Acceptance:

- A corridor campaign produces a year of causally connected funding, operational, civilian and political developments. Coverage must extend across the game's theatres; this scenario is a verification case, not a pilot-only scope.
- Professionalisation reduces routine presidential workload while strengthening lawful institutional limits.

## 14 — Citizens, places and development that changes politics

Actions:

- Introduce recurring households, workers and businesses driven by actual prices, access, employment, security, power and service outcomes.
- Let people organise, petition, relocate, change employment or support alternatives. Avoid one uniform voice of the public.
- Model representative economic groups: salaried households, informal traders, farmers, manufacturers and import-dependent businesses.
- Distinguish educational access/completion/quality, health access/outcomes, coverage/reliability, affordability/fiscal durability and distribution/national output.
- Make rival policy approaches capable of different successes and recognisable failures, with adequate financing and implementation.
- Connect ports, corridors, industrial clusters, power networks and service catchments to project performance and beneficiaries.
- Let development create new constituencies: taxpayers demand accountability, firms seek credit and entry, workers organise, graduates expect jobs and capable agencies resist misuse.
- Support wealthy-but-unequal, capable-central, decentralised and politically open governing outcomes without making one universal score the only legitimate ambition.

Acceptance:

- National improvement can coexist with a specific group losing out, with reasons visible.
- Later administrations face new political questions caused by earlier successes, not only stronger versions of the same crisis.

## 15 — Maintenance, implementation diagnosis and real opposition

Actions:

- Separate construction from operation; create staffing, servicing, supply and renewal obligations with adequate delegation.
- Escalate maintenance only when a material change requires presidential judgement.
- Diagnose design failure, funding delay, incapable execution, obstruction and external shocks separately. Remedies must address the relevant cause.
- Give opponents programmes, constituencies and specific proposals. Allow them to be right, to defend a neglected group and to campaign for particular reversals.
- Support adopting, negotiating, defeating or demonstrating an alternative to an opposition proposal.

Acceptance:

- Replacing a minister does not magically fix missing funding. A functioning asset reduces old problems while requiring intelligible upkeep.
- Opposition strategy is more than an inverse approval gauge.

## 16 — Succession openings, playable aftermath and transferable countries

Actions:

- Make succession a strategy pursued while governing, not only a replacement setup after the ending. Allow grooming several credible candidates, testing them through delegated responsibility, negotiating endorsements and choosing when to declare support.
- Separate successor competence, electoral appeal, coalition acceptance, personal loyalty and commitment to particular policies. A loyal incompetent heir and a capable independent successor must produce different risks and rewards.
- Make preparation matter through the party nomination, campaign, constitutional process and transfer of authority. Use the actual party and political history, not a hardcoded player-party assumption. Endorsement improves prospects but cannot guarantee a win.
- Store a negotiated succession settlement naming policies to preserve, appointments or coalitions to respect, unfinished work to complete and institutional protections. Distinguish public programme commitments from private expectations or improper protection bargains.
- Reward a well-played succession with a government able to maintain functioning assets, complete commitments and sustain legitimate reforms. Credit the originating administration without giving the former President automatic control over the successor.
- Give successors independent ambitions and evidence-based reasons to honour, reinterpret, renegotiate or break particular terms. Do not make betrayal a compulsory twist or blind loyalty the only successful outcome.
- Let the player negotiate continuity with a rival, accept an independently successful heir, mishandle a nomination, lose influence through overreach or establish institutions that preserve a legacy without a loyal individual.
- Preserve supported patronage networks and obligations, with explicit distinctions between personal favours and obligations of the state. Failed succession can leave factions, exposed bargains and contested legacy decisions rather than merely reset relationships.
- Make repeated presidencies transform the inherited political landscape. Succession success is judged through continuity, delivery, institutional survival, legitimacy and the successor's own results, separately from the predecessor's personal safety and influence.
- Build history-derived succession dossiers: delivered/reversed/abandoned policies, funded obligations, institutional independence, powerful beneficiaries and electoral promises.
- Differentiate heirs, victorious rivals and replacements after disgrace. Include public handovers and private letters where supported by history.
- Offer consequential preservation, investigation, renegotiation or rupture toward the predecessor's settlement.
- Implement a bounded playable post-office chapter using remaining networks, investigations, legacy defence, settlements and rebuilding influence.
- Support complicated legacies: an honest former leader can undermine a successor; a corrupt former leader can defend a valuable institution.
- Implement versioned country export/import with a structured handover letter for asynchronous human succession. The predecessor's interpretation remains separate from verified state.
- Preserve deterministic seed/state, schema compatibility, integrity checks and unsupported-version explanations.

Acceptance:

- Preparation during office measurably changes nomination support, transition conditions and prospects for policy survival. Its effects are explained to the player and recorded in history.
- A competent independent successor can count as a successful legacy. A protected former President whose programme collapses cannot be described as an unqualified succession success.
- A well-prepared heir, failed heir and negotiated rival handover lead to distinct inherited commitments, relationships and post-office possibilities.
- Another player can inherit a completed exported country and encounter its real obligations and disputed history.
- Losing office opens meaningful decisions rather than merely ending the record.

## 17 — History book, interface, scenarios and shareable outcomes

Actions:

- Turn the archive into causal histories of institutions, assets, agreements and citizens.
- Distinguish announcement, authorisation, financing and delivery; originating versus completing administrations; prediction versus observation; allegation versus established event.
- Add attributed government, press, opposition and citizen interpretations of a shared verified history.
- Reflect accumulated history through office documents, objects and changes in presentation, with an appropriately different post-office setting.
- Make briefings lead to a short explained action shortlist tied to urgent problems, coalition stability and preparation; retain the complete toolbox.
- Build guided first-month decisions and consequence explanations without locking experienced players into a tutorial.
- Add short authored scenarios, deterministic daily seeds, replayable shared starting conditions and shareable verdicts/history extracts grounded in actual outcomes.
- Keep views concise and accessible. Routine progress belongs on commitments and status screens.

Acceptance:

- A player can explain why a current dispute exists and trace it through earlier decisions.
- Shared verdicts and history extracts do not invent outcomes or erase misconduct.

## 18 — Balance, complete verification and documentation

Actions:

- Update simulator strategies to exercise rival policies, reversals, recruitment, negotiated settlements, favours, emergency financing, military missions and maintenance.
- Retain comparisons across reformer, information-checking/trusting, machine, institutionalist, populist and kleptocratic programmes, with transparent script limitations.
- Investigate dominance rather than enforcing equal re-election rates. Corruption must have credible immediate benefits, dependencies and later risks.
- Add regression checks for reproduced defects and state invariants, not tests that merely restate implementation.
- Run type checking, content lint, appropriate scenario checks, multi-seed balance simulations and multi-government runs.
- Browser-playtest every opening, core monthly play, team recruitment, negotiations, emergency finance, military, operating assets, succession, post-office play and imports/exports. Check responsive layout and accessibility.
- Refresh README counts, GDD chapters, opening descriptions, handoff/deployment status and all affected player-facing wording. Resolve missing-headline warnings or explicitly justify intentional blanks.
- Produce a completion record mapping every plan requirement to implementation, evidence and remaining failures. All requirements must be satisfied or clearly reported as blocked; do not silently reclassify work as future scope.

## Required connected acceptance histories

1. **Industrial transformation across three governments:** power and a privileged investment deal create factories and a patron; a successor confronts procurement opening and an investigation; a third government inherits workers, new firms and competing interests. Follow the institution, location, minister, citizens and archive throughout.
2. **The Reformer’s Handover opening:** contested achievements, costly continuity, coalition pressures, deliberate team selection and a first consequential file.
3. **Refusal and settlement:** a specific request is refused, closes, develops through a different response and can be settled with explicit bilateral favour terms.
4. **A payroll liquidity crisis:** timed asset sales, tax-debt collection, savings and borrowing resolve or fail to resolve the shortage with accurate ownership and obligations.
5. **A currency shock:** reserves constrain intervention; actual exchange-rate exposure changes foreign debt and investment valuations; export operations and import needs feed the external account.
6. **A military corridor mission:** resources, intelligence, command, civilian protection, procurement and political pressure evolve over a year.
7. **Exceptional recruitment under pressure:** a recruit accepts conditions, delivers a distinctive capability and reacts coherently when those conditions are breached.
8. **Independent institution versus its founder:** a justified adverse action, legal contest and continuing independence affect the founder and successor.
9. **Public services with competing approaches:** university and health policies produce observed access, quality and affordability outcomes, including uneven beneficiaries.
10. **Defeat and handover:** playable post-office choices, complete historical accountability and transfer of the country to another human player.
11. **Succession earned through play:** compare similar starting countries with deliberate candidate development, coalition negotiation and institutional protection versus neglect or coercive overreach. Verify that preparation changes the transition and legacy without guaranteeing victory or controlling the successor.

The redesign is complete when these connected histories and all workstream requirements work in the actual game, survive saves and succession, and have been checked through simulation and human play. Additional prose, larger bonuses or a reorganised menu cannot stand in for that behaviour.
