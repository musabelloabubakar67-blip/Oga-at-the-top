## Scene quality standard

For every retained or new scene answer: why this situation, why now, why this person, what judgement is required, what persists, how the consequence returns, what changes on recurrence and what closes the episode?

Distinguish a decision/escalation from an unchanged condition, progress update or closing report. Repetition identity includes the subject and episode. Meaningful scenes can include sensible committees, useful negotiation, good-faith disagreement and clean transactions; predictable futility is not the only satire.

Examples to enforce:

- A six-month written target actually returns for evaluation, recognising resources withheld by the government.
- An empty reserve cannot be released again.
- A sold asset is no longer wholly owned, reusable or earning all its old income.
- A successful power or corridor investment removes old constraints and creates different decisions.
- A distinguished appointment enables a specific capability and has stated, enforceable terms.
- An inherited policy, abuse or investigation is acknowledged accurately even when politically disputed.
