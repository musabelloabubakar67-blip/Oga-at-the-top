# Playtest findings
1. Certificate never mentions the running mate; the VP appears unannounced.
2. senate.screening "Carry the Senate along": text and favour are Maigari (sen_pres), standing fx goes to Zango (sen_approp).
3. Below the fold the page background is white: the right column overruns the viewport, "Your record" sits on white, nearly invisible.
4. Orders page is ~7,300px tall: the Finance replacement candidate list is always expanded (≈12 candidates), most marked "will not take it" at low national integrity. Collapse it; fold refusers.
5. Budget opens 1 point over the limit (last year's allocation, eroded by inflation); Send is disabled until the player trims. Offer 'trim to fit'.
6. Disabled buttons explain themselves only in hover tooltips (e.g. VP work, many others): invisible on touch and easy to miss.
7. "It can be brought back in 1 months" plural.
8. Election night: per-state result squeezed into a narrow column ("LOST · 37–" / "57") with the row empty.
9. Lost re-election 35.6–64.4 at 47% approval: landslide not explained by approval; investigate.
10. The declaration explains only the day's swing; it should list every cause of the margin (approval, machine, scandal, united opposition, rival, money, governors, rallies, chest).
11. Verdict: "capacity 34, integrity 25 → 49, 43" reads badly; should pair each figure with its arrow.
12. Verdict: "Lost the presidential election" appears among achievements in "What you will be remembered for".
13. MAJOR: governing as the party that won (PAM), its own leader Malumfashi is still listed as your rival from PAM; the defeated PSC has no rival. Rival for the new President's party should become the defeated party.
14. MAJOR: a new President (especially from the other party) starts with the defeated government's original cabinet and Villa staff. Form a fresh cabinet from the talent pool.

## Fixed in the follow-up
- Orders page: standing groups, In person and the institutions fold to one line each with how many can be used now; time-limited options stay open; a switch opens every group. About 1,300px on the first day, from 5,900.
- Disabled buttons print their reason in the button itself (one style rule covers every screen); duplicate reason lines removed.
- The Finance trio on a successor's certificate marks who served the last government; from the other side they will not serve.
- Empty Supreme Court seats: after four months the backlog is news and each vacancy costs the establishment every month.
- Also noticed during the playtest but not first logged, now fixed:
  - Advisers scored new borrowing as free and all weighed approval alike, so the Finance Minister recommended keeping the subsidy on borrowed money. Debt now counts against an option, savings for it, and each adviser weighs by brief (Finance by the books, the political adviser by the crowd).
  - "5 of 6 governors with you" fell to 3 in a month because three governors started exactly on the 58 line; nobody now starts within two points of a mood boundary.
  - Verdict "approval 50% → 50%" is judged without the six-point honeymoon; it now says so.
  - The first month's desk said "Since last month"; it says "On the first day".
  - A verdict row lost its right alignment when its figures wrapped.
